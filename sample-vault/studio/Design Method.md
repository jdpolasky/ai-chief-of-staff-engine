# Design Method

How a job moves through the studio, start to finish.

1. Site walk and notes, by hand, on paper.
2. Survey ordered if the work is grade-sensitive.
3. Concept sketches, then a water-budget run for anything irrigated.
4. Plant palette pulled from the plant database, names copied exactly.
5. CAD master file, one per site, with survey, grading, planting, irrigation
   layers.
6. Client or board review by flattened PDF.
7. Build, with planting timed to the dormant season.

Paper first, CAD second. The drawing is the record; the design is what the
drawing says.
