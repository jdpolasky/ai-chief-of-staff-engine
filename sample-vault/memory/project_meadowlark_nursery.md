Meadowlark is a half-acre native-plant nursery Maya runs behind her studio. It
started as a way to grow out hard-to-source meadow species for her own designs
and has turned into a modest revenue line: she sells plugs and one-gallon stock
to two local installers and at a spring plant sale.

Most of the current stock is being grown for the [[project-larkspur-gardens]]
meadow conversion, so the nursery and the flagship job are scheduled together.
The species list mirrors [[reference-plantbase-db]] tags for the meadow palette.

Open items: the spring sale date is not set, propagation labels need reprinting,
and the cold frame's heater is unreliable. Friday afternoons are the standing
nursery block.

**Why:** Shows a non-design revenue stream and ties the nursery schedule to the
Larkspur planting window.
**How to apply:** Nursery tasks belong on Fridays; do not interleave them with
field or studio days.
