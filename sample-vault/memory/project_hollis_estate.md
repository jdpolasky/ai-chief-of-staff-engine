The Hollis estate is a private four-acre hillside lot above the gorge. The
clients want a terraced garden, a small orchard, and a rain-garden system that
handles runoff from the long driveway. They are unhurried and detail-oriented,
which suits Maya; the margin is good and the timeline is soft.

Design direction so far: three dry-stone terraces stepping down from the house,
an orchard of cold-hardy apple and pear on the flat bench, and a rain garden at
the driveway's low point sized off the same water model used at
[[project-larkspur-gardens]] (see [[project-rivulet-tool]]).

Open items: confirm the well's draw-down before committing to the orchard
irrigation, and get the property-line survey from
[[reference-skyline-drone-survey]] before staking terraces. The clients travel
often, so approvals can lag two or three weeks.

**Why:** Second active build; shares the water-modeling toolchain with Larkspur.
**How to apply:** Hollis decisions are rarely urgent; do not treat their slow
replies as a stall.
