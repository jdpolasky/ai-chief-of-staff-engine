Skyline is the drone-survey vendor Maya contracts for topographic and
property-line surveys. They fly a site and deliver a point cloud plus a
contour PDF, usually within a week of a clear-weather window.

When she needs it. Grade-sensitive work waits on a Skyline confirm: the entry
path at [[project-larkspur-gardens]] and the terrace staking at
[[project-hollis-estate]] both have open survey dependencies. Their contours
also feed the runoff inputs for [[project-rivulet-tool]].

Booking is weather-dependent, so lead time is unpredictable; book early and
treat the date as soft until they confirm a flight.
