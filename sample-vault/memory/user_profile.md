Maya Okonkwo is a freelance landscape architect based in Asheville, North
Carolina. She runs a one-person studio, Verdant Line Design, and takes on three
or four built projects a year plus a steady trickle of consultations. Her
training is in ecological planting design; she is licensed in North Carolina and
Tennessee.

Maya works on paper first, then in CAD. She keeps every project in this vault:
site notes, plant lists, client correspondence, and the running decisions that
shape a design. She is not a programmer, but she is building a small irrigation
tool on the side (see [[project-rivulet-tool]]) and directs the code at the
level of "what it should do," not "how it is written."

Mornings are for fieldwork and drawing; afternoons for client calls and
admin. She prefers plain language, short answers, and being told when something
is uncertain rather than guessed at.
