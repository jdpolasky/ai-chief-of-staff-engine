description: Flag a deadline once; a second nudge on the same item reads as nagging.
type: feedback
tags: [correction, pacing]

---

Maya wants time-sensitive items surfaced exactly once, with the deadline named,
and then left alone. When the assistant re-raised the same Skyline booking
deadline in two consecutive check-ins, she said the second mention felt like
nagging and eroded her trust that she could let things go.

**Why:** Repeating a still-open reminder treats her like she forgot, when she
had already decided to handle it on her own schedule.
**How to apply:** Surface a deadline once, plainly, with the date. If she
acknowledges it, drop it. Only re-raise if the deadline itself changed or new
information makes it more urgent, and say what changed.
