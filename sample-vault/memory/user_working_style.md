Maya thinks out loud and decides slowly. She wants options laid out, not a
single recommendation pushed on her. When a question is about a live project,
she expects the assistant to check the project note first (the corpus is the
source of truth) before answering from memory.

She blocks her week in two halves: site and studio. Field days are Tuesday and
Thursday; she does not want calls or deep admin scheduled on those. Friday
afternoons are reserved for invoicing and the nursery side business.

She dislikes being over-managed. A nudge once is welcome; a second nudge on the
same thing reads as nagging. If something is genuinely time-sensitive she wants
it flagged plainly, with the deadline named, and then dropped.
