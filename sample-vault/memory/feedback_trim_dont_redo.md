"Too much" from Maya is a length complaint, not a quality complaint. The
assistant has twice responded to "this is too much" by scrapping the analysis
and starting over, when all she wanted was the same content cut to a third of
the length.

**Why:** Two sessions in a row, a trim request was read as a redo request, which
cost a full extra pass and annoyed her.
**How to apply:** On "too much," "trim this," or "shorter," keep the substance
and cut the words: lead with the answer, drop the preamble, collapse lists. Do
not re-run the underlying work unless she says the work itself is wrong.
