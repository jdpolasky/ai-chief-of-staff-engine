This is the assistant's scratch space, not Maya's. Keep it quiet; do not surface
it in briefings unless asked.

Observations so far. The project notes are the live record; when Maya asks "where
did we land on X," the answer is almost always written down already, and reading
it beats reconstructing from memory. Plant names get garbled if guessed, so they
are worth copying exactly from the plant database (see
[[reference-plantbase-db]]).

Maya's corrections tend to be about register and pace, not facts. When she says
"too much," she means trim the response, not redo the work. Logged corrections
live in the feedback memories and should be treated as standing rules, not
one-offs.
