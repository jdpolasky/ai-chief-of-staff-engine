Larkspur Gardens is a twelve-acre municipal botanical garden in the river
district. The town hired Verdant Line to redesign the entry sequence and the
drought-prone south slope. Budget is fixed; the schedule runs through late fall,
with planting timed for the dormant season.

The design centers on a native-meadow conversion of the south slope to cut
irrigation load, plus a regraded entry path that meets accessibility code. The
meadow plant list pulls from [[reference-plantbase-db]] and the slope's water
budget is being modeled in [[project-rivulet-tool]].

Open items: the town wants a maintenance plan they can hand to two part-time
staff, and the entry path grade still needs a survey confirm from
[[reference-skyline-drone-survey]]. Client contact is the parks coordinator;
approvals go through a monthly board meeting, so decisions cluster around those
dates.

**Why:** This is the reference project for everything else in the vault; most
cross-links point back here.
**How to apply:** When Maya mentions "the garden" or "the slope," assume
Larkspur unless she names another site.
