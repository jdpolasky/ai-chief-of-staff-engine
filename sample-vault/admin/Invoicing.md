# Invoicing

Billing runs Friday afternoons. One invoice per project milestone, net thirty.

Open: the Larkspur board is billed monthly against the contract schedule, so the
next invoice lines up with the third-Tuesday meeting. Hollis bills per phase as
terraces and orchard are approved, which is slower and uneven.

Keep the nursery sales separate from design invoicing; they are a different
revenue line and a different tax bucket.
