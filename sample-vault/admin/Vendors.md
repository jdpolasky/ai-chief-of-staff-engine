# Vendors

The short list of people Maya hires out to. She keeps it small on purpose.

- Drone survey: Skyline. Topography and property lines, weather-dependent lead
  time, book early.
- Stone: a local dry-stone mason for the Hollis terraces, booked by the season.
- Soil and amendments: the regional landscape-supply yard, delivered by the
  truckload for the meadow conversion.

Plant material she mostly grows herself at the nursery; outside stock comes from
two wholesale growers when her own beds fall short.
