# Cycle 1 — Autumn pipeline and nursery stock

## The one sentence

Build a full autumn workload now, so the studio is not scrambling for paid work
when summer ends.

## Goals

### Goal 1: Land two design-build clients for autumn

**Why now:** The autumn install window books up by August. If the conversations do
not start in May, the two slots go to whoever asked first, and that is not the
studio. Two signed projects covers the autumn revenue gap.

**Lead measures** (countable weekly actions):
- Outreach conversations with prospective clients, target 5 per week
- Portfolio case study shipped (written up and posted), target 1 per week

### Goal 2: Get the native-plant nursery stock system running

**Why now:** Every project reuses the same forty species, and right now the
counts live in Maya's head, so she over-orders some and runs short on others. A
simple stock list with reorder points ends the guessing before the autumn rush.

**Lead measures:**
- Species entered into the stock list with a reorder point, target 8 per week
- Supplier price checks logged, target 2 per week
